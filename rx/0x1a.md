The client never handles this type; it **sends** it, to say it is leaving this server. It's sent from one place:
the [OnSendToServer](/functions/OnSendToServer) handler (`0x1ba408c`), right after it logs
"Go to gameserver: server: {}, port: {}, doorID: {}" and before it connects to the new address.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `type` | 0x1a |
| everything else | 0 |

Sent reliable, no extra data. There is no other sender in 5.60: quitting the game or leaving a world doesn't send
it (those use [action|quit](/actions/quit) and [action|quit_to_exit](/actions/quit_to_exit)).

<small>Sender: inline in `0x1ba408c` (send at `0x1ba44a4`).</small>
